Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

Formora is a form builder operated by Onu- অণু ("we", "us"). It lets people (admins) create forms, share them by link, collect responses from respondents, and generate reports and PDFs. This policy explains what data we handle and why. Questions: onu.creates@gmail.com.

2. Two different roles

  • Admins create an account and own their forms and responses.
  • Respondents fill in a form through a link. The admin who built the form decides what to ask and why. For respondent data, we only host and process it on the admin's behalf. If you are a respondent and want to access, correct or delete your answers, please contact the form owner first.

3. What we collect

  • Admin account: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in hashed form; we cannot read them. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name and email. We also generate one-time verification codes that are sent to your email.
  • Form content: form titles, fields, company name, address, logo, contact details and footer text that an admin enters.
  • Responses: whatever a respondent enters in a form (text, choices, dates, uploaded photos, signature areas), the submission time, and a PDF copy of the submission.
  • Technical data: a login session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Our hosting and database providers may log IP addresses and request details for security and reliability.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

4. How we use it

  • To create and secure accounts, send verification and password-reset codes.
  • To store forms, show responses to the form owner, and generate reports (PDF and CSV).
  • To prevent spam and abuse, and to keep the service working.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Who can see your data

Admin data and responses are protected by access rules so that each admin can only see their own forms and responses. A published form is visible to anyone who has its link, but responses are not. PDF copies are stored in a private location readable only by the form owner. Our team does not read responses except where needed to operate, secure or fix the service or to meet a legal duty.

6. Service providers

We use trusted providers to run Formora:

  • Supabase for the database, authentication and file storage.
  • An email provider to deliver verification and reset codes.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Content delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr) that serve the code libraries used by this site; they can see your IP address when your browser loads them.

Data is processed in the region where our database is hosted, which may be outside your country.

7. Retention and deletion

Admins can delete a form at any time. This permanently deletes the form, all its responses and the saved PDF copies. Your account and its data are kept until you ask us to delete them. To delete your account, contact us. Short-lived backups may remain for a limited time after deletion.

8. Admin responsibilities

If you collect personal data through a Formora form, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to do so, telling respondents what you collect and why, and handling their requests. Please do not collect sensitive data (for example government ID numbers, payment card numbers or health records) unless you really need to and have taken the necessary steps to protect it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules in the database. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict some processing. To use these rights for your admin account, contact us. For data you gave in someone else's form, contact that form's owner.

11. Children

Formora is not intended for children under 13, and admin accounts are for adults. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced on the site.
